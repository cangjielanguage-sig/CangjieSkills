This directory serves as a comprehensive resource for understanding and implementing linguistic testing, a vital component of the app globalization process. It details the procedures where local experts meticulously inspect interfaces after localization to ensure they meet local usage habits and cultural expectations. The content emphasizes the profound impact that translation quality, consistency, and style have on user retention and overall satisfaction. Furthermore, it addresses the critical need to screen for sensitive or prohibited geopolitical terms to avoid significant business risks. This guide is specifically tailored for product managers and localization teams who are preparing applications for international markets and seeking to provide a seamless, high-quality global user experience. By identifying and rectifying linguistic issues before launch, teams can safeguard their brand reputation and ensure successful global expansion. Ultimately, this documentation aims to bridge the gap between technical localization and cultural acceptance.
