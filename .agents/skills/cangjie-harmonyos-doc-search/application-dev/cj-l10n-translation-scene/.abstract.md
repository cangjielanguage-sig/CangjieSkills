This directory serves as a specialized resource center focused on the critical practice of defining and providing translation scenes within the localization lifecycle. It addresses the common challenge of ambiguity in translation by emphasizing the absolute necessity of comprehensive context information. The content covers the fundamental principles of scene creation, detailing how proper context leads to more accurate and culturally relevant localization results. Specifically, it explores two primary methodologies for conveying this information: the technical implementation of annotations directly inside string resource files and the visual support provided by interface screenshots. Furthermore, it outlines the specific data points required for effective annotations, including context definitions, parts of speech, key term glossaries, maximum text length constraints, and variable formatting details. This documentation is primarily designed for software developers and localization managers who are responsible for the preparation and curation of string resources. Its goal is to equip these professionals with the knowledge needed to prevent translation errors and ensure that translators have all the necessary scene information to perform their work effectively. It serves as a best practice manual for teams looking to optimize their localization pipeline.
