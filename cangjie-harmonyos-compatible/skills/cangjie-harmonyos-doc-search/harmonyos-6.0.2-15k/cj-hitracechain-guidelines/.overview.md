### 使用HiTraceChain打点仓颉/

This directory serves as a specialized technical resource for integrating HiTraceChain, a lightweight distributed tracing tool, within applications developed using the Cangjie (仓颉) language. It focuses on solving the challenges of debugging complex business flows that operate across distributed environments, such as multiple devices or processes. The documentation details the fundamental architecture of tracing, including the generation and management of chainIds, and provides a clear walkthrough of the necessary APIs. By following the step-by-step tutorials, developers will learn how to initiate and terminate traces to generate unique tracking identifiers. These identifiers are crucial for correlating logs and events across various threads, thereby significantly enhancing the efficiency of problem diagnosis. This guide is specifically designed for Cangjie developers who need to implement robust monitoring and tracing solutions to maintain high application performance and reliability.
