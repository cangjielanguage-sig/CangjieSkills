Key takeaways include specific formulas where rear camera rotation equals lens angle plus gravity direction, while front camera rotation equals lens angle minus gravity direction. The document instructs developers to monitor the SensorId.GRAVITY event to obtain x, y, z axis data for calculating device rotation.
