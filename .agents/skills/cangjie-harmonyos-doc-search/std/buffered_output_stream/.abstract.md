This directory serves as a specialized technical resource focused on the `BufferedOutputStream` class within the Cangjie programming language ecosystem. It is designed to assist developers in mastering the complexities of buffered Input/Output (I/O) operations, a critical component for building high-performance applications. The directory provides a comprehensive look at how to effectively write data to streams by utilizing buffering techniques to minimize direct system calls. It covers the entire workflow, from setting up the necessary `ByteBuffer` to understanding the lifecycle of data as it moves through the buffer to the final destination. Special attention is given to the `flush` function, explaining its role in data persistence. This content is highly suitable for Cangjie developers—both novice and experienced—who wish to deepen their understanding of stream handling, improve their code's efficiency, and ensure reliable data management in their software projects.
