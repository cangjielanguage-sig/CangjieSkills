This directory focuses on the essential practice of linguistic testing within the application globalization process. It acts as a conceptual guide for ensuring that localized software interfaces meet the high standards required for international markets. The content explains how local experts inspect applications post-localization to verify compliance with regional habits and cultural norms. It highlights the critical influence of translation quality, consistency, and style on user experience, alongside the necessity of managing sensitive and taboo words to avoid geopolitical risks. By emphasizing rigorous testing protocols, the guide aims to prevent user churn and facilitate safe market entry. This resource is specifically designed for product managers, localization teams, and QA professionals who are responsible for the successful deployment of international applications.
