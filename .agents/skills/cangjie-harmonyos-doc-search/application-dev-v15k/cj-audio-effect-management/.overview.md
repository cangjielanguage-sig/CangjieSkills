# cj-audio-effect-management

This directory serves as a comprehensive technical resource dedicated to the management of audio effects within the Cangjie development environment. It specifically focuses on utilizing the AudioKit framework to provide developers with robust tools for audio manipulation. The content covers the entire lifecycle of audio effect control, ranging from querying global effect modes tailored for specific stream usage scenarios to managing effects within individual playback instances. Key technical concepts detailed here include AudioStreamManager, AudioRenderer, StreamUsage, and AudioEffectMode (such as EFFECT_NONE and EFFECT_DEFAULT). This documentation is primarily designed for Cangjie developers and audio engineers who need to dynamically configure audio settings, handle complex exceptions, and ensure optimal sound quality across various playback contexts. By leveraging these resources, developers can integrate sophisticated audio capabilities into their applications efficiently, ensuring a seamless auditory experience for the end-user.
