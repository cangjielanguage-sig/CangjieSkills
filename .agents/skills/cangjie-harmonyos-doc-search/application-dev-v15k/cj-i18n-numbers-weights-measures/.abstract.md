This directory serves as a comprehensive resource for the internationalization (i18n) of numerical data, weights, and measures within the Cangjie programming ecosystem. It delves into the essential practices required to adapt software for global markets, specifically focusing on the accurate formatting of numbers, currencies, and physical units to meet diverse regional expectations. The content highlights the importance of handling cultural variations in decimal separators, digit grouping, and measurement systems to ensure data clarity. It is primarily designed for software developers and product designers who are building globalized applications. By mastering these localization techniques, users can prevent costly data misinterpretations and ensure their software delivers a seamless, professional experience to international users.
