This directory provides a comprehensive conceptual overview of Bluetooth technology, specifically tailored for developers engaged in service development. It covers the core technical principles, including the use of 2.4GHz radio waves and frequency hopping spread spectrum (FHSS) mechanisms. The material distinguishes between the two primary classifications: traditional Bluetooth (BR/EDR) and Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE). It explains how traditional Bluetooth supports higher speeds and complex protocols for audio and data, whereas BLE is optimized for minimal power consumption in IoT and wearable contexts. This resource is ideal for developers seeking foundational knowledge to understand the distinctions between Bluetooth standards and select the appropriate technology for their specific application scenarios. By mastering these concepts, developers can better architect solutions that balance performance and energy efficiency.
