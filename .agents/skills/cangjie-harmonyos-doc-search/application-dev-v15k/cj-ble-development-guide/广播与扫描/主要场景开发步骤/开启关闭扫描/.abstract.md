This directory focuses on the implementation of Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) scanning functionality, specifically the processes of starting and stopping device discovery. It covers the complete lifecycle of BLE scanning, including system prerequisites, scan management, and the critical parsing of raw advertising data packets. Key topics include the BleScanManager class, scan filters, and extracting specific data like service UUIDs and manufacturer information. The content is tailored for application developers working on HarmonyOS, OpenHarmony, or ArkTS environments who need to integrate robust Bluetooth connectivity and data interpretation into their applications.
