This directory outlines the essential development steps for implementing Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) scenarios within the HarmonyOS ecosystem. It focuses on the core interactions between GATT servers and clients, utilizing the Cangjie language and ArkTS. The content comprehensively covers the complete lifecycle of Bluetooth communication, ranging from server initialization and service management to client connection and data exchange. It is designed for software developers who possess a foundational understanding of Bluetooth protocols and asynchronous programming and are looking to build robust, efficient connectivity features in their applications.
