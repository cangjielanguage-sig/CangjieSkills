This repository serves as a comprehensive technical reference dedicated to the intricate logic of camera rotation within mobile development environments. It addresses the multifaceted challenges developers face when aligning camera previews and captured images with the user's expected orientation. The content delves into the nuances of device orientation states, the interpretation of gravity sensor data, and the specific hardware variations such as lens installation angles that differ between manufacturers. By providing clear definitions and calculation methodologies, it helps bridge the gap between raw sensor output and the final display coordinates. This resource is specifically designed for mobile application developers, systems engineers, and technical leads who are building or maintaining camera functionalities and require a deep understanding of orientation mathematics to ensure a seamless user experience across various devices and rotation scenarios.
