# 数字与度量衡国际化

This directory serves as a comprehensive resource for understanding and implementing the internationalization (i18n) of numbers, currencies, and measurements. It addresses the significant complexities involved in formatting data to meet diverse cultural and regional standards, covering essential topics such as variations in decimal separators, digit precision, and unit usage across different nations. The content bridges the gap between conceptual theory and practical application, explaining why dynamic formatting is crucial to prevent user confusion and providing specific guides for the Cangjie programming language. It is specifically designed for developers and product teams who are building global applications and need to ensure their interfaces are accurate, intuitive, and culturally appropriate for users around the world.

## Detailed Description

### 使用场景.md 使用场景.md

This document explains the necessity of formatting numbers, currencies, and measurements to accommodate diverse cultural and regional standards. It covers variations in decimal separators, digit precision, and unit usage across different nations. The text highlights a specific example where "1,000" is misinterpreted in Germany, emphasizing that fixed formats can lead to user confusion and that dynamic formatting based on locale settings is essential for accurate communication. This conceptual guide targets developers and product teams working on internationalized applications who need to ensure their interfaces align with local user habits. Keywords: internationalization, localization, number formatting, currency, measurement, cultural differences, locale.
